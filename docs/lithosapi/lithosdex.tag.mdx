Swap, provide liquidity and collect fees on the LithosDex pool.

Swaps, deposits and redemptions can be placed as **orders** under `/dex/orders`, which any miner fills for the order's executor fee, or sent **directly**, spending the pool at once. Prefer orders: a miner filling orders may replace an unconfirmed direct spend of the pool, which then has to be resent.

**All amounts are decimal strings, not JSON numbers.** A 409 means a box you quoted against has moved: re-read, re-quote and resend.
