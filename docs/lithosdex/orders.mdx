# Trading with orders

<span className="badge badge--success">Standard</span>

*For anyone trading on LithosDex. Read the [LithosDex overview](./overview.mdx) first if you have not.*

An order is how you trade on LithosDex without racing anyone for the pool. This page covers placing an
order from the web interface, what it costs, how to follow it, and how to cancel it. Deposits and
redemptions can be placed as orders too, and [Providing liquidity](./liquidity.mdx) covers what is
particular to them.

## What an order is

An order is a box on Ergo that holds the funds you are trading and states your terms. Its contract lets
exactly two things happen to it:

- **A miner fills it.** The miner trades your funds against the pool, and the contract requires you to
  receive at least your minimum. The miner keeps the executor fee and nothing else.
- **You cancel it.** Only your key can spend it this way, and your funds come back to you.

Nobody else can move the funds, and there is no time limit. An order that is never filled waits until
you cancel it.

## Placing a swap order

To place a swap order from the web interface, follow these steps:

1. Open **LithosDex**, then the **Swap** tab.
2. Check that **Use orders** is on.
3. Enter the amount you want to trade. Use the arrow between the two fields to switch between buying
   and selling LIT.
4. Choose a **Slippage** allowance. The interface turns it into the **Minimum received** written into
   your order.
5. Check the quote, then select **Place order**.

The order is placed by an ordinary transaction from your node wallet. It appears under **Your orders**
as soon as the client has sent it.

### Choosing your slippage

Your minimum is the only protection your order carries against the pool moving before it fills. Other
trades in the same block can move the price in either direction, so:

- **Too tight** and a small move leaves your order unfillable. It then waits until the price comes back,
  or until you cancel it.
- **Too loose** and your order fills at a worse price than you needed to accept.

The fill is always at the pool's actual price at that moment. The minimum is a floor, not a target, so
a looser allowance does not by itself make your trade worse.

## What an order costs and locks

The quote lists what the order needs:

| Line | What it is | When you pay it |
|---|---|---|
| **Executor fee** | What the miner who fills your order keeps. 0.003 ERG by default | Only when the order fills |
| **Network fee** | The fee on the transaction that places the order | When you place it |
| Total locked | Your trade amount plus the executor fee, held in the order box | Returned in full if you cancel |

**A cancelled order pays no executor fee.** Cancelling is a transaction of its own, so it pays an
ordinary network fee.

### Changing the default fees

The executor fee and the most of it a miner may spend on network fees come from `lithosdex.orders` in
your configuration. You can also set them for your own orders under **Settings**, with **Custom order
fees**. See [Batching and orders](../configuration/batching.mdx#fees-on-orders-you-place) for both.

**Lowering the executor fee can leave your order unfilled.** Miners skip orders paying less than their
own minimum. The default is set to clear every miner running this client with its shipped settings.

## Following an order

**Your orders** lists every outstanding order your wallet owns, with one of the following statuses:

| Status | What it means | Can you cancel? |
|---|---|---|
| **Placing** | The transaction that places it has not confirmed yet | Yes |
| **Open** | On chain and waiting for a miner | Yes |
| **Filling** | A transaction filling it is waiting to confirm | No |
| **Cancelling** | Your cancel is waiting to confirm | No |

An open order marked **Waiting for price** cannot fill against the pool as it stands, because the
pool's price is past your minimum. It fills if the price returns. Otherwise cancel it and place a new
one at the current price.

A filled order disappears from the list. What you received arrives in your node wallet.

## Cancelling an order

Select **Cancel** next to the order under **Your orders**. The client sends a transaction that returns
the order's funds to your wallet, including the executor fee.

:::note A miner may fill it first
If a miner's fill confirms before your cancel, the order is filled and your cancel fails. You then have
the result of the trade rather than your funds back. That trade still met your minimum.
:::
