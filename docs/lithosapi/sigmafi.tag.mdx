SigmaFi bonds: peer-to-peer zero-coupon loans. A borrower posts a collateral-backed order. A lender fills it, creating a bond and minting two bond-asset units: one singleton locked in the bond and one circulating claim for the lender. Repayment and liquidation burn the singleton. The borrower repays before maturity and takes the collateral back; otherwise anyone may liquidate the bond, sending the collateral less its batching fee to the vault. Whoever holds the bond asset claims what the vault holds for it.

Lists, the asset lookup and every `check` are open and sign nothing; the portfolio and everything that signs need an `api_key`. **All amounts are decimal strings, not JSON numbers.** A 409 means a box you named is already being spent: re-read and resend.
