# LithosDex

<span className="badge badge--success">Standard</span>

*For anyone trading ERG and LIT through the Lithos client. No Ergo or smart contract experience needed.*

LithosDex is a decentralized exchange for ERG and LIT. It runs entirely on Ergo smart contracts, and
your Lithos client includes a web interface for it. This page explains how a trade is priced, what it
costs, and how your trade reaches the exchange.

It does not cover depositing into the exchange, which is in
[Providing liquidity](./liquidity.mdx), or the settings a miner uses to fill other people's orders,
which are in [Batching and orders](../configuration/batching.mdx).

## How the price is set

LithosDex holds its ERG and LIT together in one **pool**, which is a single box on Ergo. You trade
against the pool, not against another person, so a trade never waits for someone to take the other
side.

The pool sets its price from its own reserves. It keeps the product of its ERG and its LIT constant
across a trade, before fees. Two things follow from that:

- **Every trade moves the price.** Buying LIT takes LIT out of the pool and puts ERG in, so the next
  LIT costs a little more.
- **Large trades move it further.** The bigger your trade is against the reserves, the worse your
  average price. The web interface shows the price you will get before you confirm.

The reserves belong to **liquidity providers**, who deposit ERG and LIT together and earn the fees
trades pay.

## What a trade costs

Every swap pays about 0.3% of what you put in, in two equal parts:

| Fee | Share of your input | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Pool fee | 0.15% | Stays in the reserves, which raises the value of every provider's share |
| Permanent fee | 0.15% | Set aside for providers, who claim it separately |

On top of that you pay:

- **A network fee** on the transaction you send, as with any Ergo transaction.
- **An executor fee**, if you trade with an order. It is paid only when a miner fills the order.

## Orders and direct trades

A trade reaches the pool in one of two ways. The **Use orders** switch in the web interface picks
between them, and it is on by default:

| | Order | Direct trade |
|---|---|---|
| What you send | An order box that holds your funds and your terms | A transaction that spends the pool itself |
| Who completes it | A miner, who fills the order against the pool | You, if your transaction lands |
| Extra cost | The executor fee, 0.003 ERG by default, paid only on fill | None |
| When several trades arrive at once | Miners fill them one after another in the same block | Only one can spend the current pool box. The rest fail and must be sent again |
| If you change your mind | Cancel the order and your funds come back | Nothing to cancel once it is sent |

**Use orders.** The pool is one box, so direct trades collide with each other and with miners. A
miner filling orders replaces any unconfirmed direct trade in the blocks it builds, and that trade then
has to be sent again against the new pool. An order does not collide: it waits in its own box until a
miner fills it at your price or better.

[Trading with orders](./orders.mdx) covers placing, following and cancelling an order.

## Who fills orders

Miners do. A Lithos miner with block transactions turned on fills LithosDex orders in the blocks it
mines, and keeps each order's executor fee. A miner can also fill orders by broadcasting them to the
network, paying the network fee out of the executor fee.

Filling is permissionless and checked by the order's own contract. A miner cannot fill your order at a
worse price than your minimum, and cannot take anything but the executor fee you set.

**An order paying less than miners accept waits.** Miners skip orders whose executor fee is below
their own minimum. The default executor fee clears the shipped minimum of every miner running this
client, so leave it unless you have a reason to change it.

## Using the web interface

Open **LithosDex** in the client's navigation bar. The interface has the following tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Swap** | Trade ERG for LIT or LIT for ERG, and follow or cancel your orders |
| **Liquidity** | Deposit into the pool, or close a position |
| **Flush** | Move the permanent fees the pool has collected into the vault, where providers claim them |
| **Fees** | See and claim the permanent fees your positions have earned |
| **History** | Chart the fees earned over time, for the whole pool or one position |

Anything that sends a transaction needs the client's API key. Enter it under **Settings** on the Swap
tab. The key is kept only in memory, so you enter it again after refreshing the page. **Settings** also
holds auto-refresh and your own executor fee for new orders.

:::danger Your client signs with your node wallet
LithosDex trades spend funds from the node wallet the client uses. Keep the client's port off the open
internet, and change the shipped API key. See [Node connection and security](../configuration/node.mdx).
:::
