SigmaFi bonds: peer-to-peer zero-coupon loans. A borrower posts an order with collateral, or a lender posts an offer with the principal; the other side fills or closes it, which creates a bond and mints one bond asset to the lender. The borrower repays before maturity and takes the collateral back; otherwise anyone may liquidate the bond, sending the collateral less its batching fee to the vault. Whoever holds the bond asset claims what the vault holds for it.

Lists, the asset lookup and every `check` are open and sign nothing; the portfolio and everything that signs need an `api_key`. **All amounts are decimal strings, not JSON numbers.** A 409 means a box you named is already being spent: re-read and resend.
