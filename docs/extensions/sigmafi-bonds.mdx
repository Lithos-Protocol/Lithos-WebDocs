# SigmaFi bonds

<span className="badge badge--info">Advanced</span>

*Assumes you know Ergo's UTXO model and what a token is. Start with [Mining on Lithos](../tutorial-basics/mining-on-lithos.mdx) if not.*

SigmaFi bonds are peer-to-peer loans on Ergo, backed by collateral. A borrower locks collateral, a lender
lends ERG or a token against it, and the borrower repays a fixed amount by a fixed block. If they do not,
the collateral goes to the lender. This page explains how a bond runs, how to borrow and lend from the
web panel, and what your client does with bonds on its own.

It does not cover the API endpoints behind the panel, which are in the [API reference](/api) under
**SigmaFi Bonds**, or the settings for liquidating bonds, which are in
[Batching and orders](../configuration/batching.mdx#sigmafi-bond-liquidations).

## What a bond is

Every SigmaFi bond today is a zero-coupon bond. It pays no interest along the way: the borrower repays
one amount, larger than the loan, at the end. A bond has these terms:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Loan | ERG, or an amount of one token |
| Repayment | What the borrower pays back, in the same asset as the loan |
| Maturity | The block by which the borrower must repay. Repayment closes at this block |
| Collateral | ERG and any tokens the bond holds until it is repaid or liquidated |
| Batching fee | The most of the collateral's ERG whoever liquidates the bond may keep |
| Liquidation | The rule for when the bond can be liquidated. Today, anyone from maturity on |

The difference between the repayment and the loan is the lender's return.

## How a bond is made

Either side can start:

| You want to | You create | The other side |
|---|---|---|
| Borrow | An **order**, holding your collateral and the terms you ask for | A lender fills it. The loan comes to you and your collateral moves into the bond |
| Lend | An **offer**, holding the loan and the terms you ask for | A borrower closes it by posting the collateral you asked for, and takes the loan |

An order can fix its maturity to a block, or ask for a term counted from the block a lender fills it.
A term counted from the fill does not shrink while the order waits. It must be at least 30 blocks.

Until it is filled or closed, the person who created an order or offer can cancel it and take back what
it holds.

## The bond asset

Filling an order or closing an offer mints one token to the lender: the **bond asset**. Whoever holds it
is owed the bond. You can send or sell it like any token.

Its name, `SigmaFi ZC-Bond`, and its description are set by whoever minted it, so they prove nothing.
Anyone can mint a token with the same name. The web panel and **Wallet** check each bond asset against
the chain and mark it ZC-Bond only when it is the asset its bond was created with. A token named like a
bond asset that fails the check is marked Not a ZC-Bond.

## How a bond ends

A bond ends in one of two ways, and either way the lender's side goes to a vault for the bond asset's
holder:

1. **Repaid.** Any block before maturity, the borrower pays the repayment into the vault, and the
   collateral returns to them.
2. **Liquidated.** From maturity on, anyone can liquidate the bond. The collateral goes to the vault,
   less the batching fee, which the liquidator keeps.

The holder of the bond asset then **claims** everything the vault holds for it. The claim burns the bond
asset, so every vault box for it is claimed in one transaction.

You do not need to liquidate bonds yourself. Lithos miners liquidate every matured bond that pays a
batching fee of at least 0.05 ERG, in their own blocks first and by broadcast two blocks later.

## Borrow or lend from the web panel

Open **Extensions > SigmaFi Bonds** in the web panel. It has four views:

| View | What it does | Needs your API key |
|---|---|---|
| Book | Lists orders you can lend into, offers you can borrow from, and live bonds | Only to send |
| Create | Makes an order to borrow or an offer to lend | Only to send |
| Portfolio | Shows your orders, offers, loans and bond assets, with cancel, repay, liquidate and claim | Yes |
| How it works | A short summary of this page | No |

Every action is checked by your client first and shows what you pay, what you receive and any warning
before anything is signed. A check never signs or sends anything. The send uses your client's wallet and
pays the 0.001 ERG network fee.

**Read the batching fee before you lend.** If the borrower does not repay, you receive the collateral
less up to the batching fee. Orders and offers made from your client carry at least 0.05 ERG, so Lithos
miners liquidate them. A bond paying less is left to you to liquidate from **Portfolio**.

## What can go wrong

| Situation | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The borrower misses maturity | The bond can be liquidated. The collateral goes to the vault | Claim it from **Portfolio** once it is liquidated |
| The collateral is worth less than the loan at maturity | The borrower may let the bond be liquidated | Choose collateral and terms that cover your loan |
| An order you lent into had a fee under 0.05 ERG | No Lithos miner liquidates it | Liquidate it yourself from **Portfolio** |
| You send the bond asset away | Whoever holds it is paid instead of you | Keep the bond asset until you claim |
| You claim while a liquidation is still pending | Your client refuses the claim | Claim after the liquidation confirms |
